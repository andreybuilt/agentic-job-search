# TARGETING.example.md -- Scoring rubric template

Copy this to `TARGETING.md` and adjust weights/scales to your own priorities. Score every
candidate role before building documents for it; roles below your personal cutoff don't get
built.

## Weighted criteria

| Criterion | Scale | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit (skills/experience match) | 0–10 | 3x | How closely the role matches your actual, documented experience in `FACTS.md` |
| Compensation | 0–5 | 2x | 5 = clearly above your comp floor; 0 = at or below it |
| Location / remote | 0–5 | 2x | 5 = fully remote or in your home city; 0 = relocation required with no assistance |
| Warm-path bonus | 0–3 | 1x | +points for a referral, a direct contact, or prior contact with the team |
| Staffing-agency penalty | 0 to −5 | 1x | Negative points if the posting is from a third-party staffing agency rather than the employer directly |

**Total score = sum of (criterion score × weight).** Set your own cutoff (e.g. "build documents
only for roles scoring 40+") and record it here once you've calibrated it against a few weeks of
real postings.

## Strike conditions (auto-disqualify regardless of score)

A role is struck immediately, with no score computed, if any of these are true:

- The posting requires security clearance you do not hold and cannot obtain in a reasonable
  timeframe.
- The posting names a specific coding framework as a **primary duty** when the role is
  otherwise customer-facing (a mismatch between the title/scope and the actual day-to-day work
 -- a red flag for a mis-tagged or bait-and-switch listing).
- The posted salary range tops out below your comp floor in `FACTS.md`.
- The role is below your minimum seniority level as stated in your own targeting notes.
