# INTERVIEW_TRACKER.example.md

> **Grace period note:** if you wire up automated rejection/acknowledgement classification
> (e.g. scanning inbound email for "unfortunately" / "not moving forward" patterns), hold a
> grace period (a few business days) before auto-closing a thread. ATS auto-rejection emails
> are sometimes followed, hours or days later, by a human-initiated interview request -- closing
> the thread the moment the automated rejection lands can cause you to miss it.
